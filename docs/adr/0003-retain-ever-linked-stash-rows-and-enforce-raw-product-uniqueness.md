# Retain ever-linked stash rows and enforce per-stash raw-product uniqueness

Stash edit/delete reversal must preserve exact source-row semantics without pushing stash identity through the diary model, so ever-linked stash items are retained as hidden zero rows instead of being hard-deleted, and raw-product rows are unique per stash on `(productId, measurementType)` with later same-key adds reviving the retained row instead of creating duplicates. The uniqueness rule is enforced in both domain logic and the database, revive/merge keeps the existing frozen snapshot metadata, caller-owned outer transactions coordinate diary and stash mutations together, and restore fails as an invariant violation if a required retained row is missing instead of silently reconstructing from generic diary data. Unlinked diary returns remain allowed, but they create synthetic raw snapshots (`productId = null`) that do not merge into identified raw-product uniqueness keys. This keeps reversal fidelity inside the stash domain, avoids expanding diary identity just to support stash repair, and bounds retained raw-product growth by the per-stash uniqueness key rather than by every historical add/remove cycle.
