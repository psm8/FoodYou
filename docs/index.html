Food You onboarding

Use stash and shopping flows faster

Food You now makes it easier to add products, recipes, and one-off items to your stash without breaking your normal routine. This guide walks through the new paths so you know what to tap, what each flow is best for, and what to expect after saving.

Add existing products Reuse the familiar search flow to add items from your food catalog straight into a stash.
Build shopping sessions Keep search visible while you assemble several products before committing them together.
Save recipe snapshots Turn a recipe into a stash item with its own stored nutrition and amount.
Quick add one-off items Capture a manual stash item without creating a reusable product or recipe first.

What each flow helps with

Unified Add for products Open Add from Home Stash or Stash Management when the food already exists in your catalog. Pick the product, choose the measurement you already know, and save it to the target stash.
Shopping session Use this when you are stocking up and want to add several products in one pass. Search stays available while you build the draft.
Recipe snapshot Choose a recipe from the same Add experience when you want to save a prepared batch into stash with a fixed amount and fixed nutrition.
Manual Quick add Use Quick add for homemade, unpackaged, or temporary items that you want in stash now, without adding them to your reusable food catalog.

How to use the new stash flows

1

Start from the stash you want to update

Open Home Stash or Stash Management, then pick the stash that should receive the item. If you use more than one stash, the target stays explicit during the flow.

2

Choose the flow that matches the task

  • Add for an existing product from your food catalog.
  • Add then choose a recipe when you want a stash snapshot from a saved recipe.
  • Quick add when the item is one-off and should not become a reusable catalog entry.
  • Shopping session when you want to build a multi-item draft before confirming.
3

Enter the amount using familiar measurement language

Product and shopping flows use the same style of measurement picker you already see elsewhere in the app. That keeps amounts easier to enter and easier to review before saving.

4

Save and keep moving

Once you confirm, the item is saved into stash and becomes available for later consumption, adjustment, movement, or merging under normal stash rules. These actions do not change your diary or calorie balance until you actually consume the item.

Shopping session walkthrough

The shopping session flow now keeps the search-first experience front and center. You can stay in the search view, choose a product, adjust its amount in the composer, and continue adding more items without losing your place.

What to expect after saving

Repeated adds can combine cleanly When you add the same item into the same stash again, Food You can keep the stash view tidy while still preserving the underlying movement history.
Recipe snapshots stay stable A stash item created from a recipe keeps the saved nutrition and amount for that snapshot, even if you later edit the original recipe.
Quick add stays out of your catalog One-off stash items do not turn into reusable product or recipe entries unless you create those separately on purpose.
Diary totals stay unchanged Stash intake records inventory only. Nutrition counts apply when the item is consumed later.

Next steps

Need to stock a stash quickly? Start a shopping session from Stash Management, add several products, then confirm the whole draft when you are ready.
Need to save leftovers or a homemade batch? Use recipe snapshot when the recipe already exists, or Quick add when it is a one-off item that should stay local to stash.
